# GCSE Higher question-bank import

974 questions added from the remaining 44 papers. Together with the approved 23-question June 2024 Paper 1 pilot, the bank contains 997 GCSE Higher questions across 45 papers. All 72 pre-existing Further Maths records and all pilot records are unchanged. No Foundation questions were invented.

## Extraction

Original PDF content is embedded as vector crops. Numbered questions and all continuation pages are identified from question-start and total-marks markers. Content bands preserve wording, subparts, marks, diagrams, tables and required grids; unused vertical space and dotted answer lines are removed. Adjacent content bands are assembled in source order. Long questions retain multiple pages; answer labels and total marks are kept with preceding content. The approved pilot files are unchanged.

Classification uses the six agreed GCSE groups, with descriptive subtopics. Initial text-based classification was reviewed and corrected for ambiguous cases. Visual checking was sample-based, not a manual proofreading of every question.

## Verification

- Every paper has a contiguous question sequence and totals 80 marks.
- Unique IDs; every question PDF loads and every new PDF renders.
- All 45 source PDFs match the supplied archive byte for byte.
- Existing 95 index records preserved exactly.
- Samples include the first and longest question from each new paper, across P1/P2/P3, June/November and multiple years. Required graphs, tables and drawing grids were visually checked.
- Automated suite: 75 tests pass.
- Chrome: combined filters, Select All deduplication and preservation, previews, existing Further Maths selection/export, touch and responsive regression checks pass.
- Large real-data export: 233 Algebra questions, standard A4 portrait pages.
- Mixed-paper booklet checked visually, including short questions, graphs, multi-page questions and the approved pilot.
